# Instrument and sample licences

JV Studio currently contains nine original Tone.js oscillator/envelope presets: JV Poly Synth, Basic Piano, Soft Piano, Strings, Bass, Synth Pad, Lead Synth, Choir Pad and Drum Synth. They are generated in the browser and contain no third-party samples, recorded voices or SoundFonts.

Any future instrument asset must document its name, author, source, licence, redistribution permission, modification permission, commercial-use permission and review date before inclusion.
